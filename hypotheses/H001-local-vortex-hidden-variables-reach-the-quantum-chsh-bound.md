# H001 — Local vortex hidden variables reach the quantum CHSH bound

Origin: [`_archive/lineage/nodes/bell-tests.md`](../_archive/lineage/nodes/bell-tests.md).
Theory: [`T009`](../theories/T009-the-vortex-electron-vs-bell.md).

## The claim

The friction-vortex local hidden-variable model reproduces the quantum CHSH
correlation, so |S| exceeds the local bound of 2 and approaches 2√2 ≈ 2.83.

## What refuted it

The node's declared kill condition: CHSH |S| stays at or below 2 in the Monte
Carlo. It does, exactly at 2. The single-particle spin result
(`vortex-single-particle-spin`) stays supported inside principia and is not this
claim.
