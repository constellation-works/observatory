# H006 — Swirl modes of the vortex electron are photons

Origin: [`_archive/lineage/nodes/swirl-photon.md`](../_archive/lineage/nodes/swirl-photon.md).
Theory:
[`T007`](../theories/T007-the-swirl-ball-electron-and-the-photon-correspondence.md).

## The claim

The swirl mode of the vortex electron is quantised as photons, and its far field
matches the classical dipole.

## What would refute it

The node's kill condition: a swirl mode whose quanta are not photons, or a far
field that does not match the classical dipole. Neither has been found; the
far-field match is the part actually checked, which is why the strongest entry
below is `strong` rather than decisive.
