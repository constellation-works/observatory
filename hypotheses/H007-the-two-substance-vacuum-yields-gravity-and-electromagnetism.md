## The claim

In a two-substance vortex vacuum — particles as substrate defects, charge as
winding — both a gravitational sector and the electromagnetic sign are derivable
from a source law in the packet Hamiltonian.

## What would refute it

The node's kill condition, either half: no gravitational sector derivable from a
source law in the packet Hamiltonian, or the electromagnetic sign lost in 3-D.
The first half has fired once already (the defect-gravity lattice), while the
packet sector holds up, which is why the log below disagrees with itself and the
status is `inconclusive` rather than either verdict. Disagreeing entries coexist
on purpose.
