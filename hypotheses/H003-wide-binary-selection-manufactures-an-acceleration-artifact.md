# H003 — Wide-binary selection manufactures an acceleration artifact

Origin:
[`_archive/lineage/nodes/wide-binary-selection-methodology.md`](../_archive/lineage/nodes/wide-binary-selection-methodology.md).
Theory: [`T010`](../theories/T010-wide-binary-selection-methodology.md).

## The claim

Geometry and truncation in wide-binary sample selection manufacture an
acceleration-dependent artifact, so a reported deviation from Newtonian
prediction at low acceleration can be produced by the selection alone.

## What would refute it

The node's kill condition: the preregistered control shows no injected-effect
power difference between selected and unselected samples. The control has not
delivered that comparison — four of its arms failed, and the failure is
diagnosed rather than tuned away, which is why this stays open in substance and
`inconclusive` in status.
