# Equivalence-principle tests: Eötvös to MICROSCOPE, and the composition of mass

The measured wall around any theory whose gravitational charge is not exactly total energy:
composition-dependence of free fall (the weak equivalence principle, WEP), quantified by
the Eötvös parameter η(A,B) = 2|a_A − a_B|/(a_A + a_B) for two test bodies of different
composition. Companion fact-cluster: what the mass of ordinary matter is actually *made
of* — because that decomposition is what a WEP violation would have to key into. These
bind kill condition A of the two-substance vortex vacuum
([theory/two-substance-vortex-vacuum](../theory/two-substance-vortex-vacuum/), ORB-10160).

The binding bound is MICROSCOPE: any composition-dependent coupling of gravity to Ti vs Pt
larger than ~3 × 10⁻¹⁵ in relative acceleration is refuted.

## What mass is made of (why composition-dependence is fatal)

The point of pairing these facts with the η bounds: the mass of ordinary matter is
overwhelmingly *dynamical field energy*, not a sum over constituent rest masses — so a
gravitational charge that counts constituents ("inventory") instead of energy misses large,
material-dependent fractions of the inertial mass.

- **The proton's mass is >90% QCD dynamics.** Lattice-QCD decomposition of the proton's
  energy–momentum tensor (Yang et al. 2018, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 121, 212001): quark kinetic
  energy 32(4)(4)%, gluon field energy 36(5)(4)%, trace anomaly 23(1)(1)%, and only
  9(2)(1)% from the quark scalar condensate (the piece tracing to Higgs-given quark
  masses). Arithmetic cross-check (ours, from PDG current-quark masses): the u,u,d current
  masses total ≈ 9–10 MeV of the proton's 938 MeV — about 1%.
- **Nuclear binding energy differs between materials.** Binding energy per nucleon spans
  ~7.5–8.8 MeV across stable nuclei (values from the AME2020 evaluation: Wang et al. 2021,
  *Chin. Phys. C* 45, 030003) — e.g. ≈ 8.72 MeV for ⁴⁸Ti vs ≈ 7.92 MeV for ¹⁹⁵Pt. As a
  fraction of rest mass (dividing by u = 931.5 MeV), Ti and Pt differ by
  **Δ(BE/A)/u ≈ 8.6 × 10⁻⁴**: nucleon *count* and nucleon *energy* part company at the
  10⁻³ level between exactly the materials MICROSCOPE flew.
- **Neutron–proton asymmetry**: m_n − m_p = 1.293 MeV (PDG), ≈ 1.4 × 10⁻³ of the nucleon
  mass, and the n/p ratio varies across materials — a second inventory-vs-energy channel
  of the same order.

**The kill arithmetic for inventory-coupled gravity** (ours, flagged as derived): a theory
whose gravitational charge counts particle inventory — nucleons, cores, defects — while
inertia tracks total energy predicts η(Ti, Pt) at the Δ(BE/A)/u level, ~9 × 10⁻⁴. Against
MICROSCOPE's 3 × 10⁻¹⁵ that is an exclusion by **about eleven orders of magnitude**; even
against the 1922 Eötvös bound it fails by five. Universality of free fall at 10⁻¹⁵ forces
the gravitational charge to track total energy to parts in 10¹².
