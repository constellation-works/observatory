## What sets U — the inherited question, sharpened

[retarded-scarcity-wake](../retarded-scarcity-wake/) § open debts already asks what defines
the substrate velocity at a real body. The closed system sharpens it uncomfortably:
substrate parcels obey the rolling rule — they free-fall — while matter virializes into
orbits. A parcel and a star that once comoved do not stay comoving; around any orbiting
body the model *generically* predicts a wind of order the local orbital/infall speeds. If
the galactic Newtonian field is river-carried, the arithmetic (from the sourced velocity
scales in [lorentz-violation-bounds](../../studies/lorentz-violation-bounds.md) § velocity
scales: solar galactic orbit ~220 km/s; CMB motion 369.82 km/s; solar river at 1 AU
~42 km/s) puts U_⊙ at O(300 km/s) — U/c ~ 10⁻³ — and Theorem 2's O(U·v_GP) photon-sector
wake effects land in the neighborhood of measured light-deflection precision. That is a
**live kill test**, now calibrated by the measured wake and
[moving-source-gravity-bounds](../../studies/moving-source-gravity-bounds.md), but not yet
decidable: the PPN reduction yields far-field coefficient slots whose fixture has not produced a
converged wall comparison. The escape routes are
themselves informative: either the local substrate comoves with local structure (needs a
mechanism the rolling rule does not provide), or the galactic field is not river-carried
(feeding the open carrier question on
[gravity-as-scarcity](../gravity-as-scarcity/) § what remains live), or the model eats a
preferred-frame contradiction at O(10⁻³).

Two sharpenings from the 2026-08-21 critique stand under arbitration: (i) the
wind/gravity matching radius r_m = 2GM/U² is a few solar radii at U ~ 300 km/s, so
grazing-limb deflection measurements live *in the matching region*, not in a clean
wind-dominated far field — far-field wake multipoles from a half-width-12 box are not
yet the right observable objects; (ii) even if the galactic field is not river-carried,
the *local* orbital winds remain — Earth moves at ~30 km/s through the Sun's river
(~42 km/s at 1 AU), binary components through each other's wells — so the moving-source
problem stands at U/c ~ 10⁻⁴ in every bound system, independent of the galactic-carrier
question. Whether those winds are in tension with preferred-frame bounds is *not* a raw
U/c comparison: the sourced walls now exist
([moving-source-gravity-bounds](../../studies/moving-source-gravity-bounds.md)) and the PPN
reduction is executed
([ppn-reduction-of-the-settled-flow](../ppn-reduction-of-the-settled-flow/)) — what the
comparison still awaits is that doc's far-field slot-coefficient fixture.
