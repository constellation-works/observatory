## The S2 disjunction — fifth force or metric medium (ORB-10161)

Kill condition A left S2 without a gravitational job: gravity is the polarity-blind energy
monopole, and S2's displacement bookkeeping does not gravitate. The discussion thread closed
on the two remaining candidates — S2 as a feeble finite-range **fifth force** riding over
gravity (Branch A), or S2 as the **metric medium** itself, the substrate whose excitations
are matter and light (Branch B) — mutually exclusive, observably distinct. This section
derives both from the condensate mechanics. The disjunction does not survive the derivation
symmetrically: Branch A is refuted analytically on measured anchors (a three-blade squeeze),
the merger its consistency conditions force is derived, and Branch B survives as the only
reading — with a sharp new equation-of-state requirement and a clean bill from every current
redshift instrument.

### Branch A — S2 as a finite-range fifth force

**(A1) The form, its parameters, and the sourcing fork.** The mechanics does imply a Yukawa,
so the discussion's guess is earned rather than assumed: linearize the S2 sector about its
uniform density, and a static, localized S2 disturbance is screened over the S2 healing
length — the static response kernel is (−∇² + O(1)·ξ₂⁻²)δn₂ ∝ source — so the mediated
potential between two S2-displacing defects is ∝ e^(−r/ξ₂)/r with range set by ξ₂ (ours,
from the standard linearized-GP static response; the healing length is the same dynamical
core scale as in
[studies/vortex-atoms-and-quantized-circulation](../../studies/vortex-atoms-and-quantized-circulation.md)).
Over the void/energy monopole this is exactly the canonical fifth-force parameterization
V(r) = −(GMm/r)[1 + α e^(−r/ξ₂)]
([studies/fifth-force-searches](../../studies/fifth-force-searches.md)). What the mechanics
does *not* set is the magnitudes: α (the S2-channel-to-gravity strength ratio) is free, and
ξ₂ ≈ 5.25 kpc = 1.6 × 10²⁰ m demands a healing-length hierarchy ≳ 10³⁰ over any
microphysical core scale, with no mechanism — "the finer substance heals longer" names a
direction, not a number. (A kpc healing length also makes S2 effectively non-restoring on
every laboratory scale: each nucleon's expelled-S2 disturbance is a kpc-sized, hugely
overlapping halo. Nothing forbids that; it is recorded as strange.)

The sourcing fork, each choice with its Eötvös exposure:

- **S2-specific displacement** (the ontology's natural choice: protons and neutrons expel
  S2, so the charge counts nucleons ~A): charge-to-mass then differs between materials at
  the nuclear-binding level, Δ(q/m) ≈ 8.6 × 10⁻⁴ between exactly MICROSCOPE's Ti and Pt
  ([studies/equivalence-principle-tests](../../studies/equivalence-principle-tests.md)). At the
  galactic-job strength |α| ≈ 0.68 (A2), predicted η ~ 6 × 10⁻⁴ vs the measured 3 × 10⁻¹⁵:
  **dead by ~11 orders** — the same blade as kill condition A, cutting the same way.
- **Void volume**: inventory-shaped; identical arithmetic, identical verdict.
- **Flow energy** (charge ∝ total energy = m): Eötvös-safe by construction — the only
  survivor. Its price is collected in (A6).

**(A2) The β identification, tested analytically.** Does ξ₂ ≈ β = 5.25 kpc reproduce the
fitted boost q(r)/q(R₀)?

- *Sign first.* An attractive (α > 0) Yukawa's force-boost factor
  [1 + α e^(−r/ξ₂)(1 + r/ξ₂)] decreases monotonically in r — it can only produce a *falling*
  boost, while the fitted scarcity boost rises through the band (0.70 at 5 kpc → 1.36 at
  18.75 kpc, normalized at R₀). The identification therefore requires **α < 0**: a repulsive
  component screening gravity inside ξ₂ (the laboratory G is the screened G_∞(1+α)) —
  Sanders' anti-gravity structure, which at the mediator level means a **vector** with a
  conserved charge ([studies/fifth-force-searches](../../studies/fifth-force-searches.md)).
  Note the internal tension with (A1): the Eötvös-safe sourcing (energy) wants a scalar,
  whose sign is wrong for the job.
- *Shape.* Matching the Yukawa boost to the fitted scarcity boost in value and slope at R₀
  (point-mass kernel; local log-gradient βF(R₀)/R₀² ≈ 0.066/kpc) fixes α ≈ −0.68 at
  ξ₂ = 5.25 kpc. The shapes then diverge where data can see: the Yukawa saturates
  exponentially in r/ξ₂, the scarcity boost as exp[−βF̃/r] — a 1/r tail. Computed at the
  matched parameters: the v² boosts differ by +9% at 5 kpc, +2–4% at 12–15 kpc, +5% at
  18.75 kpc, and the far-field asymptotes differ (1.57 vs 1.73 relative to R₀) — several
  km/s in v against a fit whose RMSE is 2.7 km/s. **Verdict: same qualitative family
  (rise-and-saturate), measurably divergent in principle.** Whether the actual Gaia band
  decides was an apparatus question — filed and **since run as faraday task ORB-10167**
  ([scarcity-rotation-curve-fit](../../../orrery/lab/sims/scarcity-rotation-curve-fit/), orrery
  `8990fd3`; parent § form and universality adjudication): **the band does not decide.** The
  free Yukawa fit landed on this section's matched parameters (α = −0.679, ξ = 4.642 kpc vs
  the analytic α ≈ −0.68), RMSE 2.91 vs scarcity's 2.70 km/s; the point estimate prefers
  scarcity (ΔAIC −369.6, all 27 profile variants agreeing) but the 200-resample bootstrap
  95% interval [−503.9, +1051.9] spans zero — **shape-degenerate** per the predeclared rule.
  The 2–9% analytic divergence computed above is real but below the band's resolving power;
  the point-mass-kernel caveat stands (the disk convolution remains undone).

**(A3) The solar system: the degeneracy that dissolves Uranus — and the blade that kills
the branch.** For r ≪ ξ₂ the Yukawa force factor is [1 + α(1 − r²/2ξ₂² + …)]: a pure

seven orders below the multiplicative reading's ~6 × 10⁻⁹ differential across the system,
which itself sits ~2× above the Uranus floor. The Galaxy's own Yukawa term tides the system
as negligibly as its Newtonian term (the parent's 8 × 10⁻¹³ AU, with an O(1)-steeper
gradient). **Branch A leaves ephemerides exactly Newtonian — the ORB-10097 Uranus tension
dissolves entirely on this branch** — and the observable separating Branch A from the
parent's multiplicative rule is exactly ORB-10097's channel: a galactocentric-oriented
Uranus residual at 0.66–3.0× the floor (multiplicative) vs ~10⁻⁷ of it (Yukawa). (The
parent's side of that contrast is now conditioned: ORB-10157 measured the parent mechanic's
superposition as headroom screening, so the multiplicative Uranus signature stands only
under the fitted-boost identification — parent § superposition adjudication.)

But the rescaled-G degeneracy holds only for *massive bodies*, and that is the kill. With
ξ₂ ≫ AU the Yukawa is fully active across the solar system, so planetary ephemerides
calibrate G_∞(1+α)M☉ — while light couples to neither a scalar mediator (conformal
invariance of null geodesics) nor a vector one (the photon carries no charge), and
propagates on G_∞M☉ alone. Deflection and Shapiro delay then mismatch the
ephemeris-calibrated GR prediction by 1/(1+α) — an effective γ_eff − 1 = −2α/(1+α) — and
Cassini's 2.3 × 10⁻⁵ forces **|α| ≤ 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ for any kpc-range Yukawa, whatever it
couples to: 4.8 orders below the required 0.68**
([studies/fifth-force-searches](../../studies/fifth-force-searches.md)). This is the ORB-10156
γ logic recurring: anything that boosts massive-sector gravity without bending light
identically dies in the light sector. The massive spin-2 escape (a mediator that *does*
bend light) hits the vDVZ discontinuity, γ = 1/2 — equally dead — and a negative-coupling
spin-2 is a ghost besides. The parent's boost mechanic is exempt from this blade — whether
carried as the (since-refuted-as-derived) multiplicative q-ratio or as the measured
ORB-10157 headroom screening: its boost lives in σ itself, which light must also read on
the surviving river completion (ORB-10159(e)) — the blade cuts precisely those readings in
which dynamics and light part company.

**(A6) Clocks: the clean-clock signature, and the forced merger.** A fifth force with its
own conserved charge cannot source time dilation. The EM precedent is exact — a Coulomb
potential accelerates charges and slows no clocks — and the measured walls enforce it
generally: Galileo's eccentric-orbit test ties clock rate to the *orbit-determined*
potential at 2.5 × 10⁻⁵ of the effect, and null-redshift comparisons force every co-located

([studies/gravitational-time-dilation](../../studies/gravitational-time-dilation.md)); a

predicts **clean clocks everywhere** — its clock sector delegated wholly to ORB-10159's flow
mechanism — and that would have been its signature, had blade one not killed it
independently. The surviving sourcing (energy, from A1) buys Eötvös safety at the cost of
the signature: a scalar sourced on total energy rescales every rest energy alike, which *is*
a universal clock potential — redshift tracks dynamics again — and by (A3) the only way to
survive Cassini is to couple to light exactly as the tensor does. **Universality in all
three measured channels (free fall, light, clocks) is the definition of a metric coupling:
the consistent remainder of Branch A is not a fifth force but a massive mode of the metric
sector.** The merger is forced from three independent directions — the discussion's
universality blade, now with three edges.

**Branch A verdict: refuted as a fundamental-mediator fifth force at galactic strength — by
composition if charged, by sign/shape if scalar, by the solar-system light sector in every
case — and what survives its consistency conditions is Branch B wearing a mass term.**

### Branch B — S2 as the metric medium

**(B1) The architecture, derived — and the discussion's mechanism corrected.** S1 keeps the
signed (EM/winding) sector; S2 is the substrate whose excitations are matter and light. This
is not a new completion: it is **ORB-10159's excitation branch with the river named** — the
GP free-fall inflow is S2 streaming toward consuming defects, matter is S2 excitation riding
it (Unruh acoustic-metric family;
[studies/river-model-and-analog-gravity](../../studies/river-model-and-analog-gravity.md)), and
ORB-10159's flow-law verdict transfers verbatim: the inflow must be Gullstrand–Painlevé
(½v² = c²σ); flux-conserving and budget-per-shell flows are dead against the clock ladder.
The discussion's stated mechanism — *"local S2 density sets the local c; depletion slows it;
every process slows together"* — does **not** survive the derivation. A static index is one
function asked to serve two potentials (Φ and γΦ) and cannot hit the measured ratio — the
ORB-10156 disease with the sign flipped — and any density-dependence of the excitation
speed is bounded at parts in 10¹³ by the clock-profile ladder (B2). What delivers the clock
sector is **flow drag**: σ = ½v²/c², SR dilation against the inflow, exactly ORB-10159(b).
The density-sets-c intuition survives only as a *contaminant* whose absence is a measured
bound. Kill condition B tightens correspondingly: with S2 the one medium under everything,
the single-cone theorem stops being an EM nicety — every excitation species must share the
S2 cone or be gapped.

**(B2) The equation-of-state requirement, computed.** Steady state means continuity ties the
flow to where S2 is consumed. Vortex cores are where matter is — and consumption *at the
cores only*, in an incompressible S2, is exactly the flux-conserving point-sink flow
(v ∝ 1/r²) that the clock ladder refutes. The two-substance mechanics has exactly two ways
out, and each is a sharp statement about the S2 equation of state:

1. **Incompressible fork** (S2 density uniform): the free-fall flow demands the volumetric
   sink ∝ r^(−3/2) — ORB-10159's owed consumption law inherited verbatim, and *harder* here:
   outside a star there are no cores to do the consuming. The lattice adjudication has
   already run against the parent's two natural counting rules (ORB-10162: neither
   self-organizes toward GP; its ledger reconciliation is **ORB-10165**'s scope, not this
   doc's) — and this family's own lattice has since added the second strike (**ORB-10164**:
   a conserving substrate has no far monopole at all). The debt stands, consolidated as
   the **source-law debt** (§ lattice adjudications, three-strike convergence).
2. **Compressible fork** (consumption at cores only): continuity with the GP velocity forces
   the density profile n₂ ∝ r^(−3/2) — the river *densifies* inward, contrary to the
   depletion intuition. Two computed constraints follow:
   - *Stiffness against excitations.* If the excitation speed depends on density as
     c² ∝ n₂^κ, a static clock at r carries the anomaly (κ/2)Δln n₂ = (3κ/4)ln(r₂/r₁)
     between stations — a log profile the measured GM/r ladder does not contain. Gravity
     Probe A's 7 × 10⁻⁵ profile agreement over 10⁴ km of altitude bounds **κ ≤ 4 × 10⁻¹⁴**
     (ours, from the ladder in
     [studies/gravitational-time-dilation](../../studies/gravitational-time-dilation.md)). A
     standard GP condensate has κ = 1 (c_s² ∝ n): **dead as the S2 equation of state by
     thirteen orders.** S2's propagation speed must be *exactly* density-independent.
   - *Softness under the flow.* A pressure-bearing medium with n₂ ∝ r^(−3/2) resists its own
     infall: the fractional flow deviation is ~(3/2)c_b²r/GM for bulk response speed c_b,
     and the Galileo profile bound (2.5 × 10⁻⁵) forces **c_b ≲ 15 m/s at
     navigation-satellite radius** — while excitations propagate at c. The medium must be
     simultaneously *pressureless to its own bulk flow* and *exactly stiff to its
     excitations*: a two-speed hierarchy ≥ 10⁷. Precedent that excitation and bulk-response
     speeds decouple exists — He II's first and second sound (240 vs ~20 m/s), ³He-A's
     Fermi-point quasiparticle speeds vs its hydrodynamics
     ([studies/superfluid-vacuum-and-emergent-gauge-fields](../../studies/superfluid-vacuum-and-emergent-gauge-fields.md))
     — but the measured precedents reach ~10¹–10², not 10⁷.

   Either way: **exact Schwarzschild clock rates demand an S2 that no standard condensate
   is.** That is the equation-of-state requirement of record, alongside the photon condition
   the branch inherits (uniform excitation speed + GP flow = Schwarzschild exactly, so γ = 1
   by flow drag — ORB-10159(e), clearing the ORB-10156 floor — plus kill condition B's one
   cone).

**(B3) Galactic clock anomalies — the kill-or-live row, computed and confronted.** In

acceleration are one field (σ and c²∇σ), so **gravitational redshift tracks the

carried in the flow, it *is* the dynamical potential. Confronted with the measured rungs

stacked-cluster ~10 km/s consistent with the dynamically inferred halo potential. **Branch B

compilation task is filed: the surviving branch's prediction is degenerate with GR+DM at

trigger that revives it is any future reading in which clocks and dynamics part company.

is nothing at kiloparsec scale.** The family's S2 sector now rides entirely on Branch B's
owed items — the equation-of-state requirement (B2), the single-cone theorem (kill
