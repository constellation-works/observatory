## Canon — what this reinvents, and what it changes

Named per the house rule: canonical reinventions are named, not flattered.

| Ancestor | What it did | What killed/limited it | What this model changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kelvin vortex atoms (1867) | atoms as knotted vortex rings in the aether; motivated by exactly Daniel's opening observation — the identity of particles — with knot type as species | no quantitative spectra; ring stability; the aether died | two substances instead of one aether; species from winding pairs + composites rather than knot type; the void core as a new structural element |
| Maxwell vortex cells (1861→1865) | EM as a mechanical vortex medium; found displacement current inside the machinery, then removed the machinery | the substrate was removable — nothing it added was measurable | binds this model, doesn't inspire it: reproduce Maxwell exactly and predict beyond, or be removed |
| Bjerknes hydrodynamic forces (1870s–1906) | attraction/repulsion between pulsating bodies as an EM analogy | the sign comes out *reversed* (like attracts) — the generic fate of scalar/pressure media | inherits the risk explicitly; bets on the circulation (vector) sector to flip the sign — measured, not assumed (ORB-10163) |
| Onsager/Feynman quantized vortices (1949/1955) | circulation quantized at exactly h/m; measured (Vinen 1961; flux h/2e in superconductors) | — (this is the piece that became real physics) | supplies the existence proof for charge-as-winding: topological exactness with dynamical core energy |
| Babaev fractional-vortex confinement (2002) | one-component windings in two-component condensates are string-confined; only integer composites free | — | supplies the precedent for the proton as a confined composite (the thread's uninvited confinement story) |
| Volovik superfluid vacuum (2003) | emergent Weyl fermions, gauge fields, and metric near Fermi points — with topological protection; emergent low-energy Lorentz invariance | the metric's *dynamics* is not Einstein's; the equivalence principle must be arranged | the serious modern anchor. This model differs by: two substances (Volovik: one condensate with a structured order parameter — and the honest note that the single-cone theorem may *require* recasting "two substances" as one vacuum with a two-component order parameter); the void core as anatomy; and the claim to derive gravity-as-scarcity as the effective far field |

The parent's own canon (Nordström's fate for scalar photon sectors, the GP river, Unruh's
acoustic metrics) is inherited through
[gravity-as-scarcity](../gravity-as-scarcity/) and its studies notes rather than restated.
