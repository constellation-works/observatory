## Reason and evidence boundary

The original static light and clock sectors and fixed-length galactic universality
failed their recorded tests. The named shear-flow completion produced conditional
static successes, but its moving-source dynamics violated the proposed Bernoulli
closure and developed a cutoff-ward density trough in the tested regime. The
remaining program required further mechanisms to establish a viable excitation medium.
Daniel chose to end that program rather than pursue another repair.

This is a research-allocation decision informed by those results, not a universal
impossibility theorem for emergent gravity. Supported model properties remain supported;
refuted claims remain refuted; unresolved claims are not relabeled as disproven.
The static closure, algebraic PPN correspondences, negative results, studies, and
cataloged Orrery simulations remain available as historical evidence.

## Operational consequence

The `closed-system-moving-existence` gate is closed by retirement. There is no further
cavitation-floor repair, far-field refinement, PPN phenomenology, excitation completion,
or galaxy-fit continuation authorized for this family. Open-question lists are archival.
Reopening this family or commissioning a successor intended to continue its program
requires Daniel's explicit authorization.

The authoritative Orbit MCP reported no tasks in proposed, backlog, someday,
in-progress, review, or blocked status in `ws_principia` or `ws_orrery` during this
retirement check. No task cancellation was needed. This records the remote work queues;
the corpus changes were prepared in the local Principia checkout.

The separately registered `two-substance-vortex-vacuum` and `retarded-scarcity-wake`
families are outside this retirement's scope. They cannot use a scarcity-family debt
or historical continuation note to reopen the retired program. The two-substance
