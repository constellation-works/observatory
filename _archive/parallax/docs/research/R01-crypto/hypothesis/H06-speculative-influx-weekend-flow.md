# H06: Speculative influx and weekend flow

## Claim

A point-in-time measure of absolute speculative-investor influx changes the distribution of weekend
Bitcoin direction, volatility, or liquidity when institutional participation recedes.

## Motivation and mechanism

Smaller speculative participants may have greater price impact during thin weekend liquidity. A
higher retail share alone is insufficient because the denominator can fall while absolute retail
activity also falls.

## Predicted observables

- A frozen Friday speculative-risk score predicts weekend flow, volatility, or liquidity measures.
- Absolute small-trade activity and total activity distinguish influx from changing participant share.
- The interaction with reduced institutional liquidity is stronger than time-of-week seasonality alone.

## Plausible alternatives

Weekend amplification may arise solely from lower total liquidity, scheduled events, or crypto-native
positioning with no influx of new speculative capital.

## Baseline

Weekend and minute-of-week seasonality without a speculative-influx state.

## Rejection criteria

Reject if only relative retail share rises while absolute speculative activity does not, or if the
state predicts neither direction, volatility, nor liquidity after timing controls.
