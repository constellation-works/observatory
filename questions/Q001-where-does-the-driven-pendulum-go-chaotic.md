## The question

For the damped driven pendulum, where in the (drive amplitude, drive frequency,
damping) space does the motion lock to the drive, where does it period-double,
and where does it become aperiodic? This is established physics; the reason to
ask it here is that it is the one neutral, well-understood system in the corpus,
so it doubles as the reference against which the shared browser apparatus is
judged.

## What would answer it

A parameter sweep that reproduces the textbook bifurcation structure, with the
transitions located to stated precision, rather than a picture that looks
chaotic. [`R002`](../research/R002-pendulum-driven/) is the interactive starter
that explores the space; it settles nothing on its own and no assessment is
recorded from it.
