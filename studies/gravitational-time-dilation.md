# Gravitational time dilation: the measured ladder from Pound–Rebka to optical clocks

The established-physics wall any theory without a clock sector runs into: gravitational time
dilation is *measured*, at precisions from 10% (1960) to parts in 10⁵ of the effect (today),
across baselines from one millimeter to 10⁴ km, and it is engineering fact in GPS. In
weak-field GR the fractional rate deficit of a clock at potential Φ = −GM/r is |Φ|/c² = GM/c²r
— a pure g₀₀ effect that slow massive particles and clocks probe even though they never probe
the spatial potential γΦ (see
[scalar-gravity-ppn-constraints](scalar-gravity-ppn-constraints.md)). This note carries the
measured ladder the gravity-as-scarcity clock sector is confronted with in
[theory/gravity-as-scarcity](../theory/gravity-as-scarcity.md) (ORB-10159).

## The measured ladder

| Experiment | Baseline | Result | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pound–Rebka 1960 (¹⁵⁷Fe Mössbauer, Harvard tower) | 22.5 m | gΔh/c² = 2.46×10⁻¹⁵ confirmed to ~10% | Pound & Rebka 1960, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 4, 337 |
| Pound–Snider 1965 (same apparatus, improved) | 22.5 m | agreement to ~1% | Pound & Snider 1965, *Phys. Rev.* 140, B788 |
| Gravity Probe A 1976 (H-maser on a ballistic rocket) | 0 → 10⁴ km altitude | GM/r redshift profile tracked continuously along the trajectory; agreement to 7×10⁻⁵ | Vessot et al. 1980, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 45, 2081 |
| GPS (operational, continuous) | 20,200 km orbits | net satellite clock rate +38.6 μs/day (gravitational +45.7, second-order Doppler −7.1); fractional 4.46×10⁻¹⁰, corrected by design | Ashby 2003, *Living Rev. Relativity* 6:1 |
| Galileo 5 & 6 (eccentric orbits after launch anomaly, e ≈ 0.16) | periodic ΔΦ modulation | GM/r profile via orbital modulation; violation parameter (0.19 ± 2.48)×10⁻⁵ | Delva et al. 2018, *Phys. Rev. Lett.* 121, 231101 |
| Al⁺ optical clocks (NIST) | 33 cm of lab height | rate difference (4.1 ± 1.6)×10⁻¹⁷, consistent with gΔh/c² | Chou et al. 2010, *Science* 329, 1630 |
| Sr lattice clocks, Tokyo Skytree | 450 m | redshift confirmed; LPI violation parameter (1.4 ± 9.1)×10⁻⁵ | Takamoto et al. 2020, *Nature Photonics* 14, 411 |
| Yb lattice clocks (NIST) | — | total systematic uncertainty 1.4×10⁻¹⁸ — sensitive to ~1 cm of height | McGrew et al. 2018, *Nature* 564, 87 |
| Sr ensemble, single sample | 1 mm | redshift gradient resolved *within one atomic sample* | Bothwell et al. 2022, *Nature* 602, 420 |

Three properties of the ladder matter for theory confrontation, beyond the bare existence of
the effect:

1. **The profile is tested, not just the local gradient.** Tower and lab experiments test
   gΔh/c² (the local gradient of the potential); Gravity Probe A and the eccentric Galileo
   clocks test the *shape* Φ ∝ 1/r over ~10⁴–10⁴·⁵ km of altitude at the 10⁻⁵–10⁻⁴ level. A
   theory predicting a dilation profile other than GM/c²r (e.g. ∝ 1/r² or 1/r⁴) fails these
   even if it can be tuned to match g at one height.
2. **The magnitude is anchored to the same G and M as orbital dynamics.** GPS works because
   the clock effect and the satellite dynamics are two faces of one potential; a theory
   cannot rescale one without breaking the other.
3. **Universality (local position invariance) is separately bounded.** Clock-comparison
   tests bound *species-dependent* deviations (different atoms redshifting differently) at
   the 10⁻⁵–10⁻⁶ level of the effect (Takamoto 2020, Delva 2018 above); a strictly universal
   (species-independent) rescaling of all clock rates evades these null-redshift tests and is
   constrained only by comparisons against a reference *outside* the rescaled region —
   ephemeris/pulsar timing rather than co-located clock pairs.

## Constancy in time

A related measured anchor: the effective coupling cannot drift. Lunar laser ranging bounds
Ġ/G = (7.1 ± 7.6)×10⁻¹⁴ yr⁻¹ (Hofmann & Müller 2018, *Class. Quantum Grav.* 35, 035015).
Any mechanism whose stored potential *accumulates* on ~Gyr timescales (fractional growth
~1/t_age ≈ 2×10⁻¹⁰ yr⁻¹ for the solar system) is excluded by more than three orders of
magnitude unless it reaches a steady state.

## What this binds in our corpus

A theory whose gravitational field is a static spatial structure with **no clock mechanism**
predicts zero gravitational time dilation and is refuted by every row of the ladder above. A
theory that buys dilation from motion through a flowing medium (the river reading — see
[river-model-and-analog-gravity](river-model-and-analog-gravity.md)) must produce the
dilation factor √(1 − v²/c²) with v² = 2GM/r *exactly*, because rows 3–5 test the profile,
not only the local gradient. The confrontation lives in
[theory/gravity-as-scarcity](../theory/gravity-as-scarcity.md) (ORB-10159 section and
ledger). The redshift rungs beyond the solar neighborhood (solar disk, S2 at Sgr A*, galaxy
clusters) are in
[gravitational-redshift-astronomical](gravitational-redshift-astronomical.md).
