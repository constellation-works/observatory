# R04 launch registry

`draft` until a second pass re-verifies every row against a fetched source**; rows are usable for
design work now and not yet for primary estimation.

Class: `F` flagship launch, `P` product launch, `M` major feature, `I` incident, `$` pricing.
Confidence: `c` a provider or outlet page was fetched, `e` dated outlet only, `u` unverified.

Only `c` rows are eligible for primary estimation. `e` and `u` rows are retained because their
*absence* would silently bias the event set toward well-documented providers.

## Clustered events

These fall within 14 days of another provider's event. They support own-response estimation and
**cannot support cross-provider attribution**, because the attention move cannot be assigned.

2023-03-14 (GPT-4 / Claude, same day) · 2024-02-15 (Sora announce / Gemini 1.5 Pro, same day) ·
2024-05-13–14 (GPT-4o / Google I/O, one day apart) · 2024-12-05–11 (o1 / Sora / Gemini 2.0) ·
2025-01-20–02-05 (DeepSeek-R1 through Gemini 2.0 GA) · 2025-03-25 (4o images / Gemini 2.5 Pro, same
day) · 2025-08-05–07 (Opus 4.1 / GPT-5) · 2025-09-29–30 (Sonnet 4.5 / Sora 2) · 2025-11-12–24 (four
flagships in 13 days) · 2026-02-05, 2026-02-17–19, 2026-04-23–24, 2026-07-08–09.

Clustering is heavy and increasing. Roughly half of the flagship launches after 2025 fall in a
cluster, which is itself a finding: launch timing is strategic and not exogenous. Any cross-provider
claim must report how many isolated events it rests on.

## Cadence warnings

- **Anthropic** shipped eleven flagship releases in the fourteen months to 2026-08 — an Opus every
  six to ten weeks. Point releases are not comparable events; treat the **tier** (Opus / Sonnet /
  Fable) as the event unit, or the provider's event count alone will drive any pooled estimate.
- **OpenAI** mainline intervals over 2025-11 → 2026-07 range 29–97 days, and GPT-5.3 never shipped
  as a mainline model, so version numbers are not a reliable event index.
- **DeepSeek** upgrades its consumer product in place under unchanged names; only R1 (2025-01-20)
  and V4 (2026-04-24) are genuinely discontinuous consumer events.
- **Mistral** model launches are discrete, but Le Chat feature posts are not and several were
  announced twice.

## Verification debt

Not yet verified against a fetched source, and excluded from primary estimation until they are:
xAI's 2023–2024 events (month-level only); DeepSeek-V2's date (arXiv submission, no provider page);
Llama 3.3; several 2026 incidents that appeared in search results only and were omitted rather than
guessed.

Events were compiled by an agent pass that fetched provider pages. Three sources were re-fetched
directly at compile time; the rest carry the per-row confidence assigned during that pass. **A row
marked `c` means someone retrieved a page, not that this file's author re-read it** — which is why
the whole registry stays `draft` until the second pass.
