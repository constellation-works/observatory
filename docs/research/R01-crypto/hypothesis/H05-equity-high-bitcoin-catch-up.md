# H05: Equity-high Bitcoin catch-up

## Claim

When equities are near highs and still advancing while Bitcoin is abnormally quiet or weak,
continuing risk appetite sometimes predicts positive Bitcoin residual returns over preregistered
daily horizons.

## Motivation and mechanism

Investors may rebalance toward a lagging high-variance asset after equity gains. The behavioral story
is not required for prediction and ordinary returns cannot establish capital transfer.

## Predicted observables

- The state predicts positive residual BTC returns at specified 1-, 5-, 10-, or 20-day horizons.
- The effect is conditional on equities continuing to advance and weakens during rollover or risk-off.
- Confirmation variables improve reliability without being selected after the outcome is seen.

## Plausible alternatives

Relative momentum may persist, or equity rollover may produce broad deleveraging in which Bitcoin
behaves as a higher-beta risk asset.

## Baseline

Unconditional Bitcoin returns and a continuation or relative-momentum model.

## Rejection criteria

Reject if underperformance persists or if any apparent catch-up disappears after separating
continuing risk-on, rollover, and risk-off states.
