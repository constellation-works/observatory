# Moving sources in the closed system

**The idea (kepler, 2026-08-21 — the front that opens once the closure is fully measured).**
With ORB-10751, ORB-10932, ORB-10755, and ORB-10934 all adjudicated, the closed system
{draw-sourced counting level σ = GM/c²r, rolling rule for space a = c²∇σ, coefficient-one
shear-consumption law} is a measured structure — but every measurement so far is **static
and radial**. The oldest killer of substrate theories waits exactly one step further: a
source *moving through* the substrate. The
[retarded-scarcity-wake](../retarded-scarcity-wake/) family already mapped this territory
for a *postulated* retarded potential and found that nothing survives except an explicit
substrate equation with derived dynamics
([moving-source-field-consistency](../moving-source-field-consistency/): retardation talk is
not a model; Branch C — matter as excitations of the medium — is the only live reading).
The closed system **is** such an explicit substrate equation, and its uniform-motion
problem turns out to be partly solvable in closed form. Two exact results fall out, one
reassuring and one sharp: the wind is invisible in the *speed* field (what that means
for clocks is a relational question — Theorem 1, consequence 2), and the flow *pattern* around
a moving mass carries a wake at first order in the wind that general relativity — for
which uniform motion is gauge — has no mass-frame counterpart of: a measurable
direction-field departure, with a new named debt (consumed momentum) attached.

**Current verdict:** the wind-tunnel fixture ran (ORB-10935, faraday, 2026-08-21 — §
adjudication below) and split the front cleanly. The Galilean null and the Bernoulli
branch's lattice realization are verified at machine precision, the measured wake differs
grossly from the Galilean river comparator U + v_GP (0.46–0.73 rad RMS), and the Bernoulli speed law's
no-stagnation-point corollary gives a sharp qualitative discriminator. But the apparatus's
predeclared steady-existence measurable returned a real wrinkle: **no globally smooth
steady single-valued flow branch exists at any swept wind strength** (ladder-stable
caustics). Because the apparatus *imposes* Bernoulli (a Hamilton–Jacobi march of the speed
law) rather than letting the dynamics discover it, the theorem rows sit at `mixed`, not
`supported`: the conditional algebra is machine-verified, and its premise — that the
moving system reaches a steady irrotational state — is now the front's live question.

The dynamical-relaxation fixture then ran (ORB-10937, faraday, 2026-08-21 — second §
adjudication below) and returned **the third outcome** — the one the binary framing
missed: neither a steady state nor sustained unsteadiness, but *slow relaxation still in
progress at the feasible horizon*. The velocity sector settles (its residual meets the
predeclared criterion at every wind on the finest rung); the density sector does not —
the consumption trough around the core is still deepening at T = 60, decaying at a
U-independent rate whose e-folding time (~65) is comparable to the whole run. No steady
sample was admitted, so the Theorem 1 kill remains undelivered. One constructed-branch
result did *not* survive contact with the dynamics: the realized consumed-momentum drag
is **linear in the wind** (|∫s·v_x dV| ≈ 56·U^0.98, drag sign at every U), not the
branch's near-U-independent 20.3·U^0.098. The extended-horizon fixture (filed at this
adjudication) inherits the kill next.

A requested outside critique was then arbitrated (grok, Daniel, 2026-08-21 — § frame
critique below) and re-aimed the confrontation: mass-frame GR is exactly Schwarzschild
(uniform motion is gauge), so the boosted-GP comparator was a Galilean coordinate
picture, not a GR observable. Theorem 2 is restated with corrected order counting — the
wake/g₀ᵢ direction sector carries the first-order-in-U physics, while the speed field's
U² term is second-order and its observability is an open relational question. The clock
"cancellation" is retracted to its common-mode core, the linear drag re-read as a
fixed-strength sink (with a posture-3 timescale that is phenomenologically silent under
any cosmological substrate inertia), cavitation of the trough named a live branch kill
(predeclared into ORB-10938 by amendment), a photon-sector modulation *scale estimate*
filed (ORB-10939), and one new named obligation recorded: the **PPN reduction of the
effective metric** — raw U/c is not comparable to α₁/α₂.

The extended horizon then adjudicated (ORB-10938, faraday, 2026-08-21 — third §
adjudication below), and the premise question is answered — **against the theorems**. At
T = 600 every (U, rung) case meets the joint residual criterion: the moving closed system
*does* settle. But the settled flow is **not the Bernoulli flow**: on converged
velocity-settled samples the squared residual |v|² − U² − 2c²σ has relative RMS 0.12
(r = 3) and 0.45 (r = 5) at 41³, U/v_GP = 0.3 — orders beyond apparatus error, with the
61³ anchor independently beyond error — and the settled state carries finite, rung-stable
vorticity. **Theorem 1's closure is refuted as a description of the realized system**
(the conditional algebra stands; the dynamics decline its irrotational premise), and the
clock corollary falls with its premise. The realized direction field lands near
ORB-10935's marched branch (multipole differences 0.02–0.18) and far from boosted GP
(0.30–0.37), with no stagnation point anywhere. Two further teeth: the **cavitation kill
fired at U/v_GP = 0.3** — the trough's minimum density trends to the positivity cutoff
with candidate agreement across 33³→41³→61³, a hole in the medium around a moving mass,
voiding the excitation reading there (a finite floor survives only at U/v_GP = 1) — and
the drag law moved again at settlement: |∫s·v_x dV| = 81.2·U^1.33, superlinear,
overturning the transient linear fit and the fixed-strength-sink slogan with it.
