## Kill condition B — two substances, one light

**The generic failure.** A medium with two components carries one propagating branch per
degree of freedom: two scalar sounds (in-phase and counterflow — He II's first and second
sound, with speeds differing by an order of magnitude; studies note), two candidate rest
frames, and — if the EM mode decomposes unevenly onto the substances — polarization-
dependent propagation. Measured against that default, the photon sector is a wall of
already-lost bets unless a theorem intervenes:

- **One speed, no birefringence:** GRB polarimetry bounds the dimension-5 helicity-speed
  split at ξ < 1 × 10⁻¹⁶ (GRB 140206A; 
  [studies/lorentz-violation-bounds](../../studies/lorentz-violation-bounds.md)) — the two
  polarizations must see *one* medium to that precision.
- **No detectable rest frame, twice over:** the parent's ORB-10159 exclusion of
  substance-coupled matter (resonator bounds beat an O(1) coupling by 9–12 orders) applies
  to *each* substrate separately, and the S1–S2 **relative velocity** is a new physical
  field with no analog in the parent — it must be locked or unexcited everywhere we look.
  Energy-dependent dispersion bounds (lattice spacing ≲ 10⁸ ℓ_Pl, parity-symmetric rules
  only) inherit unchanged.
- **The inherited photon floor (ORB-10156):** the parent's static photon sector is refuted
  — every static coupling gives γ ≤ 0 against Cassini's γ − 1 = (2.1 ± 2.3) × 10⁻⁵ — and
  the only derived route back to γ = 1 is flow drag on the free-fall (GP) inflow
  (ORB-10159(e)). This model must *beat that floor, not just meet it*: its photon must
  ride a substance combination locked to the gravitating flow. If S1 and S2 fall
  differently into a mass, light acquires gravitational birefringence/double refraction —
  a qualitatively new failure mode the parent could not even express.
- **Maxwell exactly (the removability lesson):** Maxwell built EM from vortex cells in
  1861 and removed the machinery in 1865 with nothing measurable lost (studies note). The
  substrate must reproduce Maxwell's equations *exactly* in the tested regime and predict
  beyond them, or the two substances are removable scaffolding.

**The theorem the model owes.** In Volovik's superfluid-³He program this problem has a
*solution with a proof*: near a topologically protected Fermi point, the low-energy action
is forced into relativistic form — one emergent metric, one emergent gauge field, one
shared cone for all excitations — as a property of a universality class, not a tuning
([studies/superfluid-vacuum-and-emergent-gauge-fields](../../studies/superfluid-vacuum-and-emergent-gauge-fields.md)).
The analogous obligation here: **prove that the coupled two-substance vacuum has exactly
one gapless transverse vector mode, propagating at one speed on one cone shared by all
defects, coupling to defects proportionally to net winding with one universal unit.**
Until that theorem exists (or a lattice computation stands in for it), the single photon
sector is an assumption sitting exactly where generic two-fluid media measurably fail —
and where the unit-equality half of charge quantization (§(a) above) also lives.

**The existing packet Hamiltonian is not that theorem (ORB-11219).** Its continuum
linearization — frozen in
[packet-hamiltonian-linearization](packet-hamiltonian-linearization.md), compared
against
[studies/maxwell-mode-content-and-two-fluid-acoustics](../../studies/maxwell-mode-content-and-two-fluid-acoustics.md)
— is two longitudinal acoustic branches plus frozen transverse currents. Setting
\(c_+=c_-\) degenerates the two scalar cones; it does not produce two propagating
transverse Maxwell polarizations, a Gauss constraint, or Faraday's curl. The
\(\lambda=0\) control agrees with \(\lambda\neq0\) on the vacuum background at linear
order; Hessian mixing on a small background is local and does not cure the cubic's
global unboundedness. That is a failure of this scalar-density apparatus, not a no-go
for every two-component order-parameter theory. The gate remains open on a recast or a
different interaction. No gravity-wall id is reopened.
