# The two-substance vortex vacuum

**The idea (Daniel, 2026-07-12 — stated before formalizing).** The fact that electrons,
protons, neutrons, and whatever other particles come out *exactly identical* suggests there
are different types of "bulk/space" substances, and that attractive/repulsive forces are
manifestations of potential differences in these substances. An electron is a vortex
pushing out substance 1, partially filled by substance 2; a proton is the same the other
way around, with multiple vortices. Like-charge repulsion at short range is resistance
against pushing out into a denser region of the same type. An electron's center is an
"emptier" space pervaded by *neither* substance — and when an electron (emptier space)
moves around as a clump, it is literally creating voids: hence the scarcity-of-space
phenomenon, [gravity-as-scarcity](../gravity-as-scarcity/), as a *derived* effect.

This doc founds that ontology as its own theory family. It is the **candidate
microphysics under** the scarcity model: where gravity-as-scarcity postulates a depletion
field and counts it, this model says what does the depleting — and it must therefore
*derive* the parent's field as its far-field effective description, while inheriting every
measured wall the parent has already hit (the ORB-10156 photon-sector refutation as a
floor to beat, the ORB-10159 clock-sector and rest-frame constraints — doubled, since two
substances offer two candidate rest frames).

measured and refuted at the mechanic level; ORB-10941 confirms the packet common mode is a
declared-λ trailing wake, not an attached nature result; the fixed-length universality

source-law debt, and what any paid debt must deliver at kiloparsec scale is no longer a
