# The two-substance vortex vacuum

**The idea (Daniel, 2026-07-12 — stated before formalizing).** The fact that electrons,
protons, neutrons, and whatever other particles come out *exactly identical* suggests there
are different types of "bulk/space" substances, and that attractive/repulsive forces are
manifestations of potential differences in these substances. An electron is a vortex
pushing out substance 1, partially filled by substance 2; a proton is the same the other
way around, with multiple vortices. Like-charge repulsion at short range is resistance
against pushing out into a denser region of the same type. An electron's center is an
"emptier" space pervaded by *neither* substance — and when an electron (emptier space)
moves around as a clump, it is literally creating voids: hence the scarcity-of-space
phenomenon, [gravity-as-scarcity](gravity-as-scarcity.md), as a *derived* effect.

## The mechanics, formalized

1. **Substrate.** Two conserved substances S1 and S2 pervade the same space, with local
   densities n₁, n₂ sharing a capacity: the **void fraction** ∅ ≡ 1 − (n₁ + n₂)/n_max ≥ 0
   is the space pervaded by neither. Potential differences in the substances exert forces
   through pressure/density gradients (Daniel's interaction rule). The discussion thread's
   friction objection ("won't a proton's multiple vortices grind on each other?") inverts
   into a requirement: *any* substrate viscosity decays every atom, so the substances must
   be **superfluids** — with the consequence that defects feel Magnus-type forces, not
   drag, and vortices are permanent (Helmholtz–Kelvin permanence; studies note).
2. **Particles as vortex defects.** A particle is a quantized circulation defect,
   labeled by its winding pair (w₁, w₂) ∈ ℤ² — the circulation quanta it carries in each
   substance. **Polarity** is which substance the defect expels; the expelled substance's
   absence is partially backfilled by the other, and the remainder — pervaded by neither —
   is the **void core**. **Multiplicity** is real substructure: vortex energy grows ~w²
   per substance, so multiply-wound cores split into unit vortices (observed in BECs;
   studies note), and the proton's "multiple vortices" are a *bound set* — the thread's
   co-rotating triple — whose windings sum to an exact integer. The two-component
   precedent for why the constituents cannot escape exists: fractional (one-component)
   vortices are confined by linear-tension strings, only integer-summing composites are
   free (Babaev 2002; studies note) — the confinement story arriving uninvited.
3. **Assignments and bookkeeping.** Charge is the *net* winding asymmetry,
   q = e·(w₂ − w₁); the void core is polarity-blind. Electron (1, 0); positron (−1, 0);
   proton: composite with net (0, 1); neutron: **balanced expulsion**, net (1, 1) — zero
   charge, nonzero cores, nonzero void. Each winding component is separately conserved
   (topology), so the bookkeeping carries selection rules for free — worked below.
4. **Interactions.** Two-signed EM lives in the *polarity/winding* sector: like-polarity
   defects each sit in the other's expelled-substance halo, and pushing out into a denser
   region of the same type costs energy — short-range repulsion; opposite polarities
   relieve each other's displacement — attraction. The far-field sign and law are **not**
   assumed here: the canonical hydrodynamic precedent (Bjerknes) famously *reverses* the
   electrostatic sign rule (like attracts), and the model's bet — that circulation is
   vector-shaped where pressure is scalar-shaped, flipping the sign back — is exactly what
   the lattice sim measured (**ORB-10163**, § lattice adjudications): the bet paid in
   2-D — like repels, opposite attracts — under the declared relative-phase coupling.
5. **Gravity.** The void core is the one strictly one-signed object in the ontology
   (∅ ≥ 0 always, both polarities produce it), so the gravitational monopole is
   structurally polarity-blind — as the thread's own corrections force: gravity tracks
   nucleons not electron orbits (H vs D gravitate 2:1 on one shared electron cloud),
   antihydrogen falls *down* (ALPHA-g), and nothing shields it (Le Sage/Majorana bounds).
   A moving defect carries its void with it — the "moving clump of void" that would source
   the parent's scarcity flow. (Measured against, **ORB-10164**: in a conserving substrate
   the carried void carries its compensating halo, and the pair has no far field —
   § lattice adjudications.) *Which* scalar of the core actually gravitates — void
   volume or substrate energy — is kill condition A, computed below.

## The topological/dynamical split — the keep-worthy feature

The structure the whole family is built to keep: **charge is topology, mass is dynamics.**
Winding numbers are integers because the substance's circulation must close on itself
around a defect — exact regardless of core size, shape, energy, or history — while core
energy is set by healing lengths and flow configuration, free to take any value. This
split is not speculation; it is measured superfluid physics (circulation quantized at
exactly h/m, Vinen 1961; flux at exactly h/2e; core energies dynamical — studies note).
Worked against the four things it must deliver:

**(a) Exact charge quantization across the 1836× mass ratio.** Delivered in halves. The
*integer* half is structural: every |net winding| = 1 defect carries exactly one charge
quantum, whatever its core — "exact opposite winding, wildly different cores" is precisely
how the electron and proton can cancel charge to the measured neutrality of matter while
differing 1836× in mass. The mass ratio puts *no strain* on charge equality, which no
non-topological model achieves. The *unit-equality* half is owed: q = e(w₂ − w₁) assumes a
substance-1 winding couples to the EM sector with exactly the same unit as a substance-2
winding, which topology alone does not guarantee — it needs a substance-exchange symmetry
or the emergent-gauge theorem of kill condition B. (The measured wall for that half is the
electric neutrality of bulk matter, commonly quoted near 10⁻²¹ per electron-proton pair —
`conjecture — to verify`, studies note owed.)

**(b) One-signed gravity.** The void fraction is positive-definite and polarity-blind: a
scarcity *monopole* that only adds, never cancels — the right qualitative shape for
gravity, and the reason the ontology puts gravity in the void rather than in the signed
sector. Whether the void's *volume* is the coupling survives kill condition A below (it
does not; the sign structure survives, the literal coupling dies).

**(c) Two-signed EM.** Polarity gives exactly two signs, and opposite windings superpose
to zero net circulation at distance — aggregate neutrality and shieldability come out
structurally. What is *not* structural is the force law's sign and profile (the Bjerknes
reversal risk, §4 above) — measured by **ORB-10163**: sign delivered in 2-D under the
declared coupling, law 2-D-correct, the 3-D 1/r² half still open (§ lattice adjudications).

**(d) A consistent neutron.** The (1, 1) balanced defect passes every bookkeeping test the
mechanics can currently pose. Beta decay: (1,1) → (1,0) + (0,1) conserves both windings —
n → e⁻ + p is topologically allowed. Electron capture: (1,0) + (0,1) → (1,1) — allowed,
and it is how nature makes neutrons. Annihilation selection: e⁺e⁻ = (−1,0) + (1,0) → (0,0)
— allowed to vacuum; but e⁻ + p = (1,0) + (0,1) → net (1,1) ≠ (0,0) — hydrogen *cannot*
annihilate, only capture into a neutron when energetically forced. Proton vs positron:
(0,1) ≠ (−1,0) despite equal charge — proton decay to the positron is topologically
forbidden, with w₂ playing baryon number and the two-winding bookkeeping reinventing
B and L conservation as topological charges (named reinvention: Skyrme's baryon-number-as-
winding, *Proc. R. Soc. A* 260, 127 (1961) — `conjecture — to verify` as a citation, the
correspondence is qualitative). And the neutron is *elementary* here — a balanced defect,
not a bound (p + e) pair — which is forced, not optional: the composite-neutron model is
dead against the measured Bose statistics of N-14 (Heitler–Herzberg 1929; studies note).
What the mechanics does not yet contain: spin-½ and statistics for any defect, the
neutron's anomalous magnetic moment (it demands internal charge structure — the
co-rotating composite is suggestive but nothing is computed), and the neutrino, whose slot
(0, 0) is indistinguishable from vacuum. Recorded as owed, not patched.

## Kill condition A — the equivalence principle, computed

**The fork.** "A moving electron is a moving clump of void — literally creating scarcity"
reads gravity as sourced by **void volume**: an inventory-like quantity, fixed per defect
species, insensitive to how much energy the configuration stores. The alternative is that
gravity is sourced by **substrate energy** (core + flow). These forks are exhaustive up to
fine-tuning: a void volume that tracked energy universally — shrinking per joule of
binding at one fixed rate — would just *be* an energy bookkeeping in different units, i.e.
the second fork wearing the first's clothes.

**The void-volume fork, against measurement.** If each nucleon contributes a
species-fixed core void, a nucleus's gravitational charge goes as nucleon count A while
its inertial mass goes as total energy: A·m̄_N *minus binding energy*. The two part
company at the 10⁻³ level between real materials — binding energy per nucleon is
≈ 8.72 MeV for ⁴⁸Ti vs ≈ 7.92 MeV for ¹⁹⁵Pt, a fractional inertial-mass difference of
Δ(BE/A)/u ≈ **8.6 × 10⁻⁴** that the void inventory cannot see. Predicted Eötvös parameter
between exactly the materials MICROSCOPE flew: η(Ti, Pt) ~ 9 × 10⁻⁴. Measured:
η(Ti, Pt) = [−1.5 ± 2.3 (stat) ± 1.5 (syst)] × 10⁻¹⁵. **Refuted by roughly eleven orders
of magnitude** — and by five orders against the 1922 Eötvös bound alone; the n–p mass
difference and the electron fraction open independent channels of the same size
([studies/equivalence-principle-tests](../studies/equivalence-principle-tests.md)). The
QCD mass budget closes the last escape (a "smarter" per-species void): more than 90% of
the proton's own mass is field dynamics — quark kinetic energy, gluon field energy, trace
anomaly — not constituent property (Yang et al. 2018; studies note). There is no
inventory-shaped quantity that tracks mass; energy is not stored where counting can see
it.

**The energy fork, and its price.** If the gravitating charge is total substrate energy,
the equivalence principle is satisfied identically — inertia *is* excitation energy on
this ontology (the excitation reading of matter), so m_g = m_i by construction. But then
"scarcity of space" reduces to "energy density," which is the GR source term, and the
literal-void ontology is surrendered: the void core remains as EM/structural anatomy, but
gravity no longer knows about it. **The verdict for the ontology, stated plainly: the
void-as-gravitational-source reading is dead; what survives is void-as-anatomy plus
energy-as-source.**

**What this does to the bridge.** The derivation this family owes the parent — moving
defects source the scarcity field — survives only in energy form: moving *energy*
(defect cores and their flows) sources the scarcity flow. That is precisely the branch
gravity-as-scarcity was already forced onto by its own clock sector (ORB-10159: σ =
½v²/c², scarcity as the flow's kinetic energy, matter as excitations riding it). The two
adjudications — the parent's clock ladder and this model's equivalence-principle fork —
land on the *same* completion from independent directions, which is the discussion
thread's "universality blade": anything in gravity that knows particle type, substance, or
composition dies; only total energy survives as a source. Lattice-level check of which
quantity actually controls a defect's far field: **ORB-10164 — returned underdetermined
by nullity**: on a conserving lattice *no* configuration has a far field, so nothing
selects the gravitating quantity; the fork is **mooted pending the source law**
(§ lattice adjudications). The analytic verdict above is untouched — MICROSCOPE kills the
void-volume fork and energy survives by construction regardless of what the lattice
cannot yet express.
