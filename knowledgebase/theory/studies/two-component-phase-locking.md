# Two-component phase locking

Verified source: D. T. Son and M. A. Stephanov, *Domain walls of relative phase in
two-component Bose-Einstein condensates*, Phys. Rev. A **65**, 063621 (2002),
[full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0103451),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.65.063621). Checked sections II, III and V.

Without coherent conversion, separate particle conservation gives two global phase
symmetries. Their condensate has two acoustic modes. A conversion drive preserves only
the common phase symmetry and gaps the relative-phase mode (section II, equations
2.9–2.20). The relative-phase energy admits sine-Gordon walls (section III).
Vortices bounding a wall experience a confining force at separations larger than its
width (section V); the wall can decay, so absolute stability is not implied.

This is a binary-condensate result, not a theorem producing electromagnetism. Its
application to Principia's proposed charge is a separate conditional calculation in
[photon-mode obstruction](../theory/two-substance-vortex-vacuum/photon-mode-obstruction.md).
That chapter assumes a regular isotropic phase-only effective action and does not
exclude emergent gauge sectors in other phases.
