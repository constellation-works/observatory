## The topological/dynamical split — the keep-worthy feature

The structure the whole family is built to keep: **charge is topology, mass is dynamics.**
Winding numbers are integers because the substance's circulation must close on itself
around a defect — exact regardless of core size, shape, energy, or history — while core
energy is set by healing lengths and flow configuration, free to take any value. This
split is not speculation; it is measured superfluid physics (circulation quantized at
exactly h/m, Vinen 1961; flux at exactly h/2e; core energies dynamical — studies note).
Worked against the four things it must deliver:

**(a) Exact charge quantization across the 1836× mass ratio.** Delivered in halves. The
*integer* half is structural: every |net winding| = 1 defect carries exactly one charge
quantum, whatever its core — "exact opposite winding, wildly different cores" is precisely
how the electron and proton can cancel charge to the measured neutrality of matter while
differing 1836× in mass. The mass ratio puts *no strain* on charge equality, which no
non-topological model achieves. The *unit-equality* half is owed: q = e(w₂ − w₁) assumes a
substance-1 winding couples to the EM sector with exactly the same unit as a substance-2
winding, which topology alone does not guarantee — it needs a substance-exchange symmetry
or the emergent-gauge theorem of kill condition B. (The measured wall for that half is the
electric neutrality of bulk matter, commonly quoted near 10⁻²¹ per electron-proton pair —
`conjecture — to verify`, studies note owed.)

**(b) One-signed gravity.** The void fraction is positive-definite and polarity-blind: a
scarcity *monopole* that only adds, never cancels — the right qualitative shape for
gravity, and the reason the ontology puts gravity in the void rather than in the signed
sector. Whether the void's *volume* is the coupling survives kill condition A below (it
does not; the sign structure survives, the literal coupling dies).

**(c) Two-signed EM.** Polarity gives exactly two signs, and opposite windings superpose
to zero net circulation at distance — aggregate neutrality and shieldability come out
structurally. What is *not* structural is the force law's sign and profile (the Bjerknes
reversal risk, §4 above) — measured by **ORB-10163**: sign delivered in 2-D under the
declared coupling, law 2-D-correct, the 3-D 1/r² half still open (§ lattice adjudications).

**(d) A consistent neutron.** The (1, 1) balanced defect passes every bookkeeping test the
mechanics can currently pose. Beta decay: (1,1) → (1,0) + (0,1) conserves both windings —
n → e⁻ + p is topologically allowed. Electron capture: (1,0) + (0,1) → (1,1) — allowed,
and it is how nature makes neutrons. Annihilation selection: e⁺e⁻ = (−1,0) + (1,0) → (0,0)
— allowed to vacuum; but e⁻ + p = (1,0) + (0,1) → net (1,1) ≠ (0,0) — hydrogen *cannot*
annihilate, only capture into a neutron when energetically forced. Proton vs positron:
(0,1) ≠ (−1,0) despite equal charge — proton decay to the positron is topologically
forbidden, with w₂ playing baryon number and the two-winding bookkeeping reinventing
B and L conservation as topological charges (named reinvention: Skyrme's baryon-number-as-
winding, *Proc. R. Soc. A* 260, 127 (1961) — `conjecture — to verify` as a citation, the
correspondence is qualitative). And the neutron is *elementary* here — a balanced defect,
not a bound (p + e) pair — which is forced, not optional: the composite-neutron model is
dead against the measured Bose statistics of N-14 (Heitler–Herzberg 1929; studies note).
What the mechanics does not yet contain: spin-½ and statistics for any defect, the
neutron's anomalous magnetic moment (it demands internal charge structure — the
co-rotating composite is suggestive but nothing is computed), and the neutrino, whose slot
(0, 0) is indistinguishable from vacuum. Recorded as owed, not patched.
